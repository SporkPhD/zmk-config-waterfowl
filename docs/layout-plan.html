Waterfowl Layout Plan

A proposed keymap for your board, drawn on its real key positions, plus options for the two OLED screens. Nothing here is flashed yet. Pick the answers at the bottom and I'll build it.

Layers

The plan follows the idea behind Miryoku, the most widely used 36-key layout: each thumb key types something when tapped and opens a layer when held. Every layer then sits under the hand opposite the thumb you're holding. Home-row modifiers are ordered for a Mac, with Cmd under your middle finger instead of your pinky.

What changes from today

  • Calmer home-row mods. The stock version waits 200 ms and tends to trigger Shift or Cmd while you type fast. The plan uses the widely shared “timeless” settings: a 280 ms threshold, mods only trigger when the next key is on the other hand, and they never trigger in the middle of a fast run of typing.
  • Mac modifier order. Ctrl, Opt, Cmd, Shift from pinky to index, so Cmd is on the strong middle finger.
  • Thumbs do double duty. Esc, Space and Tab on the left; Enter, Backspace and Delete on the right. Holding Space, Backspace, Enter, Esc or Delete opens a layer.
  • Mac shortcuts built in. Undo, cut, copy, paste, line start and end, word jumps, tab switching, screenshots and media keys.
  • Roller and dial presses do something. Today they type 1, 2, 3 and 4.
  • Fixes. The stock backslash uses a code meant for European keyboards, and the apostrophe was buried on a layer. Both move to sensible spots.

OLED screens

Your screens are 128×64 pixels but mounted upright, so anything you put on them has to be drawn sideways in software. ZMK v0.3 can't rotate the screen itself. That rules out a lot of what's out there. Two other facts shape the choice:

  • Only the left half knows what you're doing. Layer, modifiers and typing speed live on the left half. The right half only knows its own battery and whether it's connected to the left half.
  • The right half is always on battery. An animation there keeps it awake and drains it noticeably faster. A still image costs almost nothing. Both screens switch off when you stop typing.

Recommendation

Use nice-oled on both halves. Left: layer, Mac modifier symbols, Bluetooth or USB, both batteries and a typing-speed Bongo Cat. Right: a pixel-art duck, with an animation as an option once we see what it does to the battery.

Your call

1. Arrow keys: H J K L or inverted T?

The plan puts ← ↓ ↑ → on H J K L, which keeps your fingers on the home row (Vim users know it). The alternative is a familiar inverted T on I / J K L.

2. Apostrophe or semicolon on the right pinky?

The plan puts ' there because English uses it far more. ; and : move to the Num and Sym layers. If you write a lot of code, you may prefer to keep ;.

3. Keep home-row mods?

They're the reason a 36-key board works. If they feel bad after a week even with the calmer timing, the fallback is to put Shift on a thumb.

4. What goes on each screen?

Left: an info screen, with Bongo Cat, Luna the dog, or just numbers. Right: the duck, an animation (gem, cat, spaceman, Pokémon), or a full mirror of the left screen later on.
